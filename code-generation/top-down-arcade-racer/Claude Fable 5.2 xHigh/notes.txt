- Developed in Claude Code
- The game is functional
- The game is titled "Tarmac Sprint"
- Dynamic camera that automatically zooms int/out according to the car's speed
- Optional overhead camera that follows the car's rotation
- Beautiful track, with zebras and grass traps, a zebra-patterned wall, surrounded by grass in a grid-like pattern and trees, and a grand stand
- At the start of the race, there is a countdown traffic light that turns from red to green
- The track has a visible darkened line that shows the optimal path
- The controls feel stiff and the car turns slower as it gets faster
- Completing laps is very challenging and requires using the brakes
- No handbrake
- When braking, Cars leave permanent brake marks on the track, and also (strangely) on the grass
- Functional mini map
- Speed meter
- The cars cast shadows
- The screen shakes when crashing at high speeds
- Race standings are functional
- The race ends after completing all laps
- The player car drives itself around the track after race ends
