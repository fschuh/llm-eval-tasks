- Developed in OpenCode
- The game is functional
- The controls are good and responsive
- The collision physics are a bit off, the cars slow down too much on impact instead of rebounding from each other
- Car reverse is correct
- The AI is strong, with two modes: waypoint only and waypoint + A* (in which the AI avoids other cars pretty well)
- The track design and visuals are not very good, with debug lines that overlap and can't be turned off
- There's debug lines to keep track of the laps and they can't be turned off
- The cars glow white when they collide, which feels like a debug effect
- There's a very weird circle particle effect when cars collide, which looks bad
- Race standings are functional
- The UI is a bit glitchy, with a shake effect due to the high resolution timers that count very quickly
- Completing all laps ends the race
- Lap bug is not present
