- Developed in Claude Code
- The game is functional
- Track is well designed, with walls, gravel traps, zebras and surrounded by grass and trees
- Car reverse is correct
- Good controls, but car physics is not very realistic especially the way it turns (the rear feels like it's detached from the ground)
- Collision physics are realistic and responsive
- The screen shakes when the car crashes at high speeds
- Car has handbrake, and leaves smoke and permanent tire marks on the track
- Driving on the gravel slows down car and leaves smoke trails
- Good AI, it avoids collisions and attempts to get out of the way and overtake other cars
- Functional mini map
- Functional race standings
- The race ends after completing all laps
