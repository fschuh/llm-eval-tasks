- Implemented in Claude Code
- The game is functional
- Nice visuals, the track has zebras around the corners and is surrounded by grass
- There's a mini-map showing the track and the car positions in it
- The track has dirt section spanning each side, and if the car runs into it it leaves trails of dirt smoke
- The cars leave permanent skid marks on the track
- There are two camera options: fixed rotation or follow the rotation of the car
- The controls and physics response and collision are good, but the physics are very unrealistic. 
- Cars lose track adherence at higher speeds which leads to a lot of sliding and it's very difficult to recover control.
- There's a handbrake, but it feels like the car is sliding on ice
- There's a screen shake effect when crashing hard into walls or cars
- Car reversing is correct
- The race ends after completing the last lap
- The player's car accelerates much slower than the AI cars, which makes it impossible to win the race
- I couldn't verify if the race standings work, because the player's car is always in last place due to it being slower than everybody else
