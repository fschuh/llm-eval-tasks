- Developed in Codex
- The game is functional
- It looks beautiful, with polished track art, including zebras around the track, starting grid slot lines, grandstands and even trees and grass on the ouside
- There is a nice looking UI on the top and bottom of the screen containing the race information
- The cars cast shadows on the ground
- The controls and collision physics are very responsive, though the car physics is not very realistic and feels like it slides a lot
- The car has a handbrake that can be used for tight turns
- Car reversing is correct
- The cars leave permanent skid marks on the track when braking or skidding
- The AI is pretty good and takes some skill to beat
- The race standings are functional
- The race ends after completing the last lap
